use these terms consistently; a correction to one term usually applies to

Control names shorten the group with the abbreviation in brackets, for
example "HC 1: Operating mode".

Product, standard and preset names stay as they are: Home Comfort, Eco,
Eco+, Boost, SG Ready, Smart Function, Power Guard, PV. "Comfort" is
translated. Brand names of manufacturers do not appear in labels.

Style: sentence case. European Portuguese spelling of the current orthographic agreement ("ação", "desinfeção", "ligação"). Address the user formally in the third person ("Introduza", "Verifique", "o seu"), as Home Assistant does in Portuguese.
